Sunset Runway

An original 2D flight game. Fly the rings, follow the river, land on the runway.

Up / Down arrows — climb / descend  ·  Left / Right arrows or A / D — bank & turn

W / S — throttle up / down  ·  Mouse — move to steer, click to boost

Space — level wings  ·  P — pause  ·  R — restart  ·  M — sound on / off

Three sorties in one flight: Ring Run — River Run — Final Approach. Watch fuel and damage. Land slow, low and lined up.
